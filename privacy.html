Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

CurioCat does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Everything you put into it stays on your TV.

What stays on your TV

Your cats' names and faces, their settings and preferences, play times and catches, and whether you've seen the tutorial and accepted these terms are saved only on your TV, in the app's own storage.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

The home screen tiles

When CurioCat is highlighted on your TV's home screen, it shows tiles for your cats, Zen Den and Call the cat. The tiles are made on your TV; your cats' names never leave it.

The tiles' pictures (drawings of the prey, the same for everyone) come from the CurioCat website, hosted by GitHub Pages. When your TV fetches them, GitHub can see your TV's internet address, as with any website visit. We don't receive it. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Your TV and Samsung

Your TV, its home screen and Samsung's app store are run by Samsung, under Samsung's own privacy policy. CurioCat has no control over what they collect.

Deleting your information

To delete everything CurioCat has saved, use Settings > Full reset, or uninstall CurioCat.

Children

CurioCat collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the CurioCat website.
